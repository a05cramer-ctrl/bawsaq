How a company gets listed

No approval. Your launch writes itself to the on-chain registry, and the chain decides who was first.

  1. Pick an open company

    Sixteen Leonida brands are on the board. Anything marked Open has no BAWSAQ coin yet.

  2. Launch it from here

    Connect your wallet and hit Launch it. One transaction creates the coin on pump.fun and registers it. The name is the company, for example Burger Shot; the ticker is yours.

  3. First launch owns it

    The earliest registered launch per company is the listing. Coins launched on pump.fun directly, even with the same name, never list.

The registry, on-chain

  1. Registry: . Every BAWSAQ launch sends it a 0 SOL transfer with a memo.
  2. Valid launch: the same transaction calls pump.fun's create, the new mint signs it, and the memo reads BAWSAQ|Company|mint.
  3. Owner: the earliest valid launch for each company. Later ones are ignored.
  4. Prices: market cap, 24h move and volume come from DexScreener. The index is the sum of every owner's market cap.
one transaction
  pump.fun   create("Burger Shot", "BSHOT", uri)  // mint signs
  pump.fun   buy(devBuy)                          // optional
  system     transfer(you → registry, 0 SOL)
  memo       "BAWSAQ|Burger Shot|<mint>"

board = getSignaturesForAddress(registry)

FAQ

Before you trade

Who launches the company coins?

Anyone with a Solana wallet, from this site. BAWSAQ never holds your keys or the coins; it reads the registry and lists the first launch per company.

Someone launched a coin with the same name on pump.fun. Does it count?

No. Only launches made through BAWSAQ are written to the registry, and only the first registered launch per company is listed.

Can a company be delisted?

No. Once a coin owns a ticker it keeps it, whatever its market cap does.

What does $BAWSAQ do?

Is this connected to Rockstar Games?

No. BAWSAQ is fan-made and not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Company names are used as in-game references.
